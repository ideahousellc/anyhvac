    Sling psychrometer for measurement of relative humidity.

    DescriptionSling psychrometer.JPG
    English: A sling psychrometer, showing the "wet bulb" on top. The muslin sleeve, pictured here, is used at temperatures above -10° C (14° F) and when the temperature is colder, a thin layer of ice is formed on the bulb.
    Deutsch: Schleuderpsychrometer
