## Engineering boundary

The airflow sequence is a conceptual visualization. It communicates only that, at the same represented airflow, a smaller cross-sectional area produces higher average velocity. It does not state a universal velocity or friction target, establish total system performance, certify code compliance, guarantee noise performance, or replace project-specific design work.
