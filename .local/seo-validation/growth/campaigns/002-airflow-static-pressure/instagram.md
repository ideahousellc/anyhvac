**Static pressure is not airflow.**

Illustrative example:
−0.20 in. w.c. return + 0.35 in. w.c. supply = **0.55 in. w.c. TESP magnitude**.

That result is a pressure relationship—not a CFM value or design target. Use the correct equipment data, operating condition, and manufacturer-defined test boundary when interpreting it.
