## LinkedIn

A pressure reading needs context.

Total external static pressure (TESP) does not independently establish airflow. Airflow evaluation needs the correct equipment data and operating conditions. Measured TESP is also distinct from design available static pressure (ASP).

The existing free AnyHVAC Airflow & Static Pressure Measurement guide explains these boundaries and connects measurements to practical tool inputs.
