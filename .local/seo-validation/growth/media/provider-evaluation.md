# Future creative-tool evaluation

No provider is selected or connected by this document. Before approving an image,
video, voice, music, or sound tool, George and Nova should record:

| Criterion | Required question |
| --- | --- |
| Commercial rights | May AnyHVAC use outputs commercially and indefinitely? |
| Free tier | What limits, expiry, training use, or hidden conversion applies? |
| API | Is a documented API available without weakening approval controls? |
| Automation | Can approved local briefs be processed reproducibly? |
| Output quality | Are people, equipment, instruments, labels, and motion credible? |
| Watermarks | Are watermarks required or removable under the applicable plan? |
| Attribution | Is attribution required, and in what exact form? |
| Privacy/retention | What inputs/outputs are retained, reviewed, or used for training? |
| Output ownership | Who owns or licenses generated output and derivative work? |
| Cost | What monthly, per-asset, storage, and overage costs apply? |
| Rate limits | What practical production and retry limits exist? |
| Export quality | Are resolution, codec, alpha, color, and audio exports suitable? |
| HVAC suitability | Can it represent mechanical systems without unsafe or false detail? |

Also evaluate security, account ownership, data deletion, provider stability, and
whether a lower-cost local or owner-supplied asset can meet the need. Unknown terms
or provenance block publication approval. Paid selection, account creation, and
provider connection require separate authorization.
