# Media provenance

Every external, generated, narration, music, ambient, or sound-effect asset must
record:

- `source`
- `license`
- `commercial_use`
- `attribution_required`
- `attribution_text`
- `date_acquired`
- `publication_approved`

Record the provider and prompt/brief reference where relevant, plus ownership,
retention, and privacy notes. “Free,” downloaded, generated, or royalty-free does
not by itself establish commercial permission. Unknown or incomplete provenance
means `publication_approved: false` and blocks the license/provenance quality gate.
