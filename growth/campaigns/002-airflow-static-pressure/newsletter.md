## Subject option

TESP is not airflow: a quick measurement reminder

## Preview text

How to keep static pressure, airflow, and design available static pressure distinct.

## Excerpt

Total external static pressure does not directly tell you CFM. Static pressure
helps describe the resistance the fan is working against at a particular operating
condition, while airflow describes how much air moves through the system.

The new AnyHVAC Airflow & Static Pressure Measurement Quick Reference walks through
pressure terminology, measurement instruments, velocity-and-area relationships,
traverses, capture hoods, and the limits that matter when connecting field readings
to HVAC calculations.
