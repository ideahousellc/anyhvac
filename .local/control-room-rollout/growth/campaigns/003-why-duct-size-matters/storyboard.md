# Storyboard — music-driven beat structure

- **0.0–1.0 s:** Camera already moving inside a full-size duct; airflow streaks cross frame on the opening beat. “Same airflow.”
- **1.0–3.0 s:** Walls begin closing while forward motion continues. “Smaller duct.” then “What changes?” Hook complete.
- **3.0–7.0 s:** Split comparison evolves continuously; small-side particles move faster. “Less area. Higher velocity.” `CONCEPTUAL` remains visible.
- **7.0–11.0 s:** Camera hugs the wall; animated boundary streaks and dimensional ticks enter on beats. “Friction changes too.” Nuance: same airflow, comparable duct conditions.
- **11.0–15.0 s:** Duct cross-section morphs through several unlabeled sizes. “Space. Velocity. Friction.” then “Sizing connects them.” No values imply a target.
- **15.0–20.0 s:** Camera accelerates through a circular opening; that opening becomes a calculator dial. The authentic AnyHVAC UI resolves behind it.
- **20.0–25.0 s:** Short pan/zoom across the real hydrated calculator: 5,000 CFM, 0.10 friction, round and rectangular results. “Evaluate the design point.”
- **25.0–28.0 s:** UI recedes into an AnyHVAC frame. “Size it faster.” / “Free. No signup.” / canonical URL.

Transitions begin before the prior beat ends. Music stays continuous at 120 BPM; supporting whooshes mark structural changes.
