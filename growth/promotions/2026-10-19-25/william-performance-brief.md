## William's challenge to George's five proposals

The five proposals are useful hypotheses; remove any high-performing or growth claim. Three duct-adjacent packages must serve distinct jobs: designer geometry comparison, equal-area friction misconception, and technician mean-velocity numeracy. Equal-area 12×12 and 6×24 inch ducts both have 144 in² = 1 ft² area; perimeters are 48 and 60 inches. Do not infer a precise friction ratio or confuse hydraulic diameter with friction-equivalent round diameter. Relative humidity falls during sensible warming at fixed pressure and humidity ratio with no moisture transfer; state the boundary. Mixed-air humidity ratio and enthalpy are dry-air-mass weighted; volumetric fraction is not universally mass fraction. Q=VA uses mean velocity, actual volumetric flow and consistent units: 1000 CFM / 2 ft² = 500 fpm, and / 1 ft² = 1000 fpm. These are illustrative values, never design targets or evidence of fan performance.
