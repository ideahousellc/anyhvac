## Source / license requirements

Every delivered input must record source, license, commercial-use permission,
attribution requirement/text, acquisition date, retention/privacy implications,
and generated-output ownership. Unknown provenance is not publication-ready.

Prioritize existing AnyHVAC and licensed free commercial-use photos/footage. Record
license evidence and model/property permissions where relevant. AI imagery may be
proposed but requires explicit owner authorization before generation; record that
authorization here. No asset purchase or media subscription without owner approval.
