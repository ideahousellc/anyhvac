# Campaign brief — 003-why-duct-size-matters

## Canonical asset

HVAC Duct Calculator: https://www.anyhvac.net/tools/duct-calculator

## Objective

Earn attention with a useful duct-sizing relationship, then reveal the flagship free tool that helps evaluate the task.

## Technical claims source

`components/duct-calculator/DuctSizingGuide.tsx`, `components/duct-calculator/DuctCalculator.tsx`, and `app/tools/duct-calculator/page.tsx`. No formula or design target is introduced or changed.

## Business hypothesis

**IF** HVAC viewers see a visually understandable consequence of duct sizing, **AND** receive useful technical context, **AND** the free calculator appears as the natural solution, **THEN** qualified viewers may visit AnyHVAC to use it. This is an unproven hypothesis. Later measurement: reach, engagement, retention where available, referral traffic, calculator visits where measurable, follow-on behavior, genuine subscribers, support, and revenue movement.

## Format and story

28-second vertical video, edited as evolving musical beats rather than slides. Hook: **“Same airflow. Smaller duct. What changes?”** A conceptual camera moves through narrowing duct geometry; particles accelerate to communicate higher velocity for the same airflow; wall-energy cues introduce friction implications with proper nuance. The opening transforms into an authentic capture of the actual calculator at its supported 5,000 CFM / 0.10 in. w.g. per 100 ft / 12-inch preferred-side state.

## Technical boundaries

Conceptual motion is qualitative, not a velocity scale or design recommendation. Duct size alone does not determine total system performance. No universal velocity or friction target is claimed. Calculator results are design assistance, not automatic final design.
